About this instrument

This page listens to three public feeds and draws what they report. It does not predict anything, and it does not rank suffering. Severity here is a rough, source-derived number used only to decide what is drawn larger.

Sources (live status in the Sources tab)

  • USGS earthquake feeds and the FDSN event service
  • NASA EONET v3
  • GDACS (alert levels)
  • NASA GIBS VIIRS imagery and fire detections
  • Natural Earth country borders

How to read it

  • Points fade over the 24 hours after an event ends.
  • Rings mark the most severe events in view.
  • History earthquakes are M4.5 and above; the live day includes smaller ones.
  • The same event reported by several sources is drawn once.

Limits

  • Sources lag, disagree and revise. Nothing here is official.
  • Satellite imagery is regional (about 250 m to 1 km per pixel), not street level.
  • Ocean events are labelled by the nearest country.
